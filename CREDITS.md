# Asset credits

The Day Today characters and newsroom references originate in the BBC comedy by Chris Morris and Armando Iannucci. Peter O'Hanraha-hanrahan is portrayed by Patrick Marber; the fictional news presenter is portrayed by Chris Morris. Reference: [WAR! clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3BO6GP9NMY), with [implementation notes](docs/day-today.md). The added Minecraft skins, prop artwork, crafting recipes and adapted dialogue are original. No programme audio, video or full transcript is distributed.

Sam Hyde voice clips are excerpts from **Sam Hyde's 2070 Paradigm Shift**, by **Sam Hyde / Million Dollar Extreme**. The full recording used for extraction was retrieved from the [MillionDollarExtreme2 reupload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4jRoatZizQ0). The [original upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-yFhR1fKWG0) is no longer available.

The [Wikimedia Commons source record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samhydetedtalk2070.png) documents the original video's Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license and its 2019 license review. [CC BY 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Modifications: 27 short excerpts selected using caption timing, converted to mono Vorbis, filtered, normalized for dialogue, and faded at the boundaries. Timings, source identifiers, file hash and changes are recorded in `assets/hydemod/sounds/sam/credits.json` inside the mod. These excerpts do not imply endorsement of HydeMod by the speakers or source uploaders.

The mod's textures, models, music, lightsaber effects, pipe sounds and combat cues are created by its local asset generators. Elephant Mario and related character names and visual references originate in Nintendo's Super Mario Bros. Wonder; the mod's model and textures are custom implementations. Minecraft is by Mojang Studios, and NeoForge is by the NeoForged project.
