# Newsroom references

The requested stretched twig appears in [The Day Today — WAR!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3BO6GP9NMY). The [pilot transcript archive](https://sotcaa.org/history/ukonline/hidden/thedaytoday_transcript03.html) also describes the satellite infomometers and the exaggerated news machinery. The war coverage ends with a souvenir video advertisement, reflected in the collectible VHS prop.

Peter O'Hanraha-hanrahan is the programme's unreliable economics correspondent. Chris Morris is the fictional studio presenter. HydeMod treats both as peaceful characters and writes original Minecraft-specific dialogue around field reporting, verification and ridiculous quotas. The reporter skins, item textures, recipes, animations and game mechanics are original implementations.

The Fact Bomb adapts the camera-equipped news-bomb gag as a flash that reveals hostile mobs. The twig's melting metaphor becomes extra durability wear in the Nether. The infomometer measures real Minecraft data, providing a useful counterpart to Peter's deliberately doubtful reports.

The additions are based on the WAR! clip and the reporting character references available when building this release. A specific user-supplied video transcript was not provided. The mod does not reproduce a full script or distribute programme recordings.
